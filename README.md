## Getting started

All text is stored in Markdown, and demos are written in HTML. This makes them easy to work with and requires no additional tools.

1. Fork this repository. ([How do I fork a repository?](https://doka.guide/recipes/github-new-fork/))
1. Clone your fork to your computer. ([How do I clone a repository?](https://doka.guide/recipes/github-clone-repo/))
1. Create a new branch.
1. Work on your material and commit your changes as you go.
1. Finished? Create a pull request. ([How do I create a pull request?](https://doka.guide/recipes/github-new-pull-request/))

That's it! After a quick review, your material will appear on the Doka website!
